## 1. Abstract & Axiomatic Foundation

Digital forensic investigations frequently fail in judicial settings because evidentiary claims rely on hearsay or unauthenticated server logs. The **AegisTrace Forensic Proof Model** formalizes leak attribution as a set of five interconnected cryptographic proofs:

$$\Pi = \langle \pi_{\text{origin}}, \pi_{\text{ledger}}, \pi_{\text{watermark}}, \pi_{\text{lineage}}, \pi_{\text{custody}} \rangle$$

An attribution conclusion $\mathcal{A}(R)$ (attributing a document leak to recipient $R$) is admitted **if and only if** all five proof branches are simultaneously valid and grounded in empirical evidence nodes within an acyclic dependency graph $\mathcal{G}$.

---

## 2. Proof Branch 1: Recipient Origin & Non-Repudiation ($\pi_{\text{origin}}$)

The core challenge of recipient attribution is proving that recipient $R$—and not an impersonator or a malicious system administrator—decrypted the specific instance of the document.

### Theorem 1 (Non-Repudiation of Decryption)
Let $K_R = (PK_R, SK_R)$ be the enrolled post-quantum ML-DSA-65 keypair of recipient $R$. Let $m_{\text{confirm}}$ be the canonical confirmation payload:
$$m_{\text{confirm}} = \text{AEGIS-DECRYPT-CONFIRM:v1}:D_{\text{id}}:Rel_{\text{id}}:R_{\text{id}}:S_{\text{id}}:K_{\text{id}}:E:T:C:t$$
where:
* $D_{\text{id}}$ is the document identifier.
* $Rel_{\text{id}}$ is the release hash.
* $S_{\text{id}}$ is the ephemeral session ID.
* $T$ is the unique dynamic watermark token.
* $C = \text{SHA-256}(D_{\text{id}} \parallel R_{\text{id}} \parallel T)$ is the watermark commitment.
* $t$ is the ISO-8601 UTC timestamp.

If $\text{ML-DSA-65.Verify}(PK_R, m_{\text{confirm}}, \sigma_R) = \text{True}$, and $SK_R$ was generated within a local hardware keystore (e.g. TPM 2.0 or isolated recipient enclave), then:
1. Recipient $R$ knowingly initiated and confirmed decryption of document $D_{\text{id}}$.
2. The dynamic watermark carrier embedded with token $T$ is uniquely and exclusively bound to recipient $R$'s authorized session.
3. Neither server operators nor third parties could forge $\sigma_R$ without solving the Module Learning With Errors (M-LWE) lattice problem.

## 3. Proof Branch 2: Permissioned DLT Ledger Inclusion ($\pi_{\text{ledger}}$)

To eliminate the possibility of retroactive backdating or evidence fabrication by administrators, every decryption receipt is committed to a permissioned append-only Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) block:

### Theorem 2 (Temporal Invariance & Quorum Consensus)
Let Block $B_k = \langle k, H_{k-1}, \mathcal{R}_k, \Sigma_{\text{quorum}} \rangle$ be a committed ledger block, where $\mathcal{R}_k$ is the Merkle root of receipts, and $\Sigma_{\text{quorum}}$ represents signatures from $Q$ authorized validators:
$$|\{v \in \mathcal{V} \mid \text{ML-DSA-65.Verify}(PK_v, \text{AEGIS-BLOCK-CONFIRM:} \parallel H(B_k), \sigma_v) = \text{True}\}| \ge \tau$$
where $\tau = \lceil \frac{2f+1}{3} \rceil$ is the Byzantine fault tolerance threshold.

Let $\text{Path}(r)$ be the Merkle audit path for receipt $r$. If $\text{VerifyPath}(H(r), \text{Path}(r), \mathcal{R}_k) = \text{True}$, then:
* Receipt $r$ existed at or prior to block height $k$.
* No party can retroactively insert, delete, or alter receipt $r$ without compromising $\tau$ independent post-quantum validator nodes.

## 4. Proof Branch 3: Watermark Physical & Carrier Binding ($\pi_{\text{watermark}}$)

When an unauthorized copy of a document is discovered (via scan, screen photograph, or digital file), watermark carrier extraction extracts token $T^*$:

$$\text{Extract}(\text{Artifact}) \to \langle T^*, \rho, \text{BER} \rangle$$
where $\rho$ is the normalized cross-correlation / detector confidence score and $\text{BER}$ is the bit error rate.

### Statistical False-Alarm Bound
The hypothesis $H_0$ (no watermark present; extracted bits generated by random noise) versus $H_1$ (watermark present) is tested under Neyman-Pearson criteria. For symbol length $L$ and correlation $\rho$:
$$P_{\text{fa}} = Q\left(\frac{\rho - \mu_0}{\sigma_0}\right) \le 10^{-6}$$
If $P_{\text{fa}} < 10^{-6}$ and $T^* == T$, the physical artifact is mathematically tied to token $T$ and the corresponding decryption event.

## 5. Proof Branch 4: Lineage & Downstream Boundary Proof ($\pi_{\text{lineage}}$)

In real-world leaks, documents may be re-shared, printed, or exported. AegisTrace enforces the **Honest Boundary Invariant**:

$$\text{LineageState} \in \{\text{DIRECT\_RECIPIENT}, \text{CONTROLLED\_FORWARD}, \text{LAST\_KNOWN\_HOLDER}\}$$

### Downstream Gap Theorem
If an unauthorized copy displays watermark token $T$ belonging to recipient $A$, but telemetry indicates $A$ exported the document to an unmonitored external flash drive or printer:
1. The lineage engine identifies $A$ as `LAST_KNOWN_HOLDER`.
2. The lineage object explicitly records `has_downstream_gap = True`.
3. The attribution engine is **forbidden** from fabricating a speculative downstream actor $X$. It must emit `ABSTAINED` or `LAST_KNOWN_HOLDER`, truthfully documenting that $A$ is the final verified custodian in the monitored boundary.

## 6. Proof Branch 5: Append-Only Chain of Custody ($\pi_{\text{custody}}$)

Every physical or digital manipulation of evidence from seizure to courtroom presentation is recorded in a cryptographic hash chain:

$$H_i = \text{SHA-256}(\text{AEGIS-CUSTODY:v1} \parallel H_{i-1} \parallel \text{CanonicalJSON}(Event_i))$$

Because $H_i$ depends recursively on $H_{i-1}$, any retroactive modification, reordering, insertion, or deletion of a custody record produces an irrecoverable break in the hash chain:

$$\Delta(Event_j) \implies H_k' \ne H_k \quad \forall k \ge j$$

## 7. Formal Non-Repudiation Synthesis

Combining $\pi_{\text{origin}}, \pi_{\text{ledger}}, \pi_{\text{watermark}}, \pi_{\text{lineage}}, \pi_{\text{custody}}$ produces an unbroken, post-quantum forensic proof chain:

$$\text{Artifact} \xrightarrow{\pi_{\text{watermark}}} \text{Token } T \xrightarrow{\pi_{\text{ledger}}} \text{Receipt } r \xrightarrow{\pi_{\text{origin}}} \text{Identity } R \xrightarrow{\pi_{\text{lineage}}} \text{Boundary } B \xrightarrow{\pi_{\text{custody}}} \text{Examiner Seal}$$

This proof model guarantees that AegisTrace forensic conclusions are objective, reproducible, mathematically grounded, and legally irrefutable.
