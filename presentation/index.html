Multi-Channel Evidence Fusion & Anti-Double-Counting

The Forensic Double-Counting Trap

If an engine extracts a physical watermark and calculates a Tardos score from that same watermark, naively summing both scores artificially inflates confidence and creates dangerous false convictions.

1. Transitive Lineage Bound

Enforces \(\max_{n \in \text{Tree}}(\rho_n \cdot \text{LLR}_n(c))\). Derived child signals cannot additively inflate their parent evidence.

2. Attack-Aware Reliability

Soft channel weights \(\rho_i\) dynamically scale down based on detected Bit Error Rate, SSIM, and Crop Ratio.

3. Conflict & Abstention

Contradictory evidence triggers CONFLICT; weak signals trigger INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE (Fail-Closed).

Five Courtroom-Defensible Decision States: ATTRIBUTED, NO_SIGNAL, INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE, CONFLICT, REVIEW_REQUIRED.
